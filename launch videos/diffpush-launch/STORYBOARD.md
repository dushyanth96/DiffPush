# DiffPush Launch Video Storyboard

## Beat 1: Hero Reveal (0-5s)
**Concept:** Start with a clean, focused hero section that introduces the product.
**Mood direction:** Modern, tech-savvy, confident.
**Animation choreography:** 
- Logo (DP) scales in with a bounce effect
- Title "DiffPush Launch" slides up from below
- Subtitle fades in
**Transition:** None - hard cut to next beat
**Depth layers:** BG: dark navy (#07090e), MG: title and subtitle, FG: logo

## Beat 2: Feature Cards (5-15s)
**Concept:** Showcase the key features of DiffPush with animated cards.
**Mood direction:** Clean, informative, approachable.
**Animation choreography:**
- Three feature cards slide in from different directions (left, bottom, right)
- Each card has a subtle hover effect on the background
- Cards use a glass-morphism design with backdrop blur
**Transition:** Smooth opacity crossfade to next beat
**Depth layers:** BG: dark navy (#07090e), MG: feature cards, FG: text content

## Beat 3: Product Demo (15-25s)
**Concept:** Show a quick demo of how DiffPush works in practice.
**Mood direction:** Dynamic, engaging, educational.
**Animation choreography:**
- Code snippet appears with typing animation
- Test results appear with pass/fail indicators
- GitHub commit preview shows up
**Transition:** Shader transition - "whip-pan" effect
**Depth layers:** BG: dark navy (#07090e), MG: demo elements, FG: UI controls

## Beat 4: Call to Action (25-30s)
**Concept:** End with a clear call to action and brand reinforcement.
**Mood direction:** Confident, encouraging, actionable.
**Animation choreography:**
- "Start tracking free" button scales in
- Gradient text effect for the final message
- Logo reappears with a subtle pulse
**Transition:** Smooth opacity crossfade
**Depth layers:** BG: dark navy (#07090e), MG: CTA elements, FG: logo

## Rhythm Plan
- Beat 1: Hero reveal (5s) - slow build
- Beat 2: Feature cards (10s) - steady pace  
- Beat 3: Product demo (10s) - dynamic energy
- Beat 4: CTA (5s) - strong conclusion

## SFX cues
- Beat 1: Soft whoosh sound on logo reveal
- Beat 2: Gentle "pop" sounds for card reveals
- Beat 3: Typing and test result sounds
- Beat 4: Confident chime on CTA
